# SOSProf.net — six permanent content pillars (SOSProf.net only)

Read this file only when the brand is **SOSProf.net**. It never applies to Elios Academy, Formatic Academy or any other brand: don't borrow their pillars, colours, logos, slogans or tone, and don't apply these pillars to them.

SOSProf.net is a **two-sided Tunisian educational marketplace**. Demand side: students and parents who choose teachers and support. Supply side: independent teachers, who are customers and partners in their own right, never just extras in student ads. Every piece of content serves at least one measurable business objective on one side.

## The six pillars (official editorial architecture)

| # | Pillar | Audience | Objective | Target share | Primary KPIs | Recommended formats |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| 01 | Student Problems & Relatable Content | Students, parents | Awareness, engagement, organic reach | 25% | Non-follower reach, shares | POV / Relatable, Comedy / Skit, Split Screen, Micro-Storytelling, Curiosity Hook |
| 02 | Platform Features & Solutions | Students, parents | Conversion, adoption | 20% | Website visits, registrations | Screen Recording, Feature demo, Premium Motion Graphics, Before / After, Explainer |
| 03 | Teacher Recruitment & Empowerment | Teachers | Teacher acquisition, retention | 20% | Qualified teacher applications | Teacher POV, Teacher Talk, UGC, Mini-Interview, Storytelling, Premium Promo |
| 04 | Educational Value & Free Knowledge | Students | Organic growth, saves, shares, credibility | 15% | Saves, shares | Educational Reel, Quiz / Challenge, Carousel, Mini-Lesson, Animated Explainer |
| 05 | Trust, Social Proof & Community | Students, parents, teachers | Credibility, trust, community | 10% | Enquiries, engagement | Testimonials, UGC, Behind the Scenes, Teacher Interviews, Community demos |
| 06 | Parents, Guidance & Educational Decisions | Parents | Trust, consideration, conversion | 10% | Parent enquiries, conversions | Parent POV, Expert Advice, Micro-Storytelling, Problem / Solution, Explainer |

Pillar rules:
- **01:** relatable daily problems (tutoring frustrations, finding the right teacher, rigid schedules, transport, unanswered homework, crowded sessions, stress, student–parent misunderstandings). Entertaining, never insulting teachers, parents or schools.
- **02:** one benefit per post (teacher choice, flexible scheduling, Q&A community/Forum, recordings per platform terms, resources, online sessions, price discussion where supported, teacher profiles). **Only launched features.** If you aren't sure a feature is live on app.sosprof.net, mark it "verify before publishing" and never present a planned feature as available.
- **03:** independence, flexible schedule, visibility, teacher profile, reaching new students, community, eligible monetisation, transparent collaboration. **Never promise earnings, students or outcomes.**
- **04:** genuine learning value, pedagogically correct and aligned with the Tunisian curriculum (Bac tips, common mistakes, mini-lessons, maths, algorithmics/Python, physics, languages, methods). Not disguised advertising.
- **05:** real teachers, real demos, real community threads. **Never invent testimonials, reviews, results or experiences**; testimonials only with permission. Blur private data.
- **06:** support choices, revision organisation, learning difficulties, motivation, school/tutoring balance, online learning. No fear-based marketing, no guaranteed results.

## Pillars are not formats
A pillar is the strategic subject and business objective. A format is the creative execution. Any compatible format can serve any pillar. The ten formats stay available independently: Split Screen, POV / Relatable, Micro-Storytelling, Premium Motion Graphics, Screen Recording, Comedy / Skit, Curiosity Hook, Quiz / Challenge, UGC / Teacher Talk, Behind the Scenes. Never create a pillar per format. Avoid the same format twice in a row in a calendar.

Example: pillar 01 · format Comedy / Skit · topic: parents can't answer a homework question · hook "تسأل بوك... يقلّك اسأل أمك!" · solution: the SOSProf.net community · CTA: discover the platform.

## Idea template (every SOSProf.net idea)
Content ID (SOS-P<pillar>-<nn>) · Title · Primary pillar (one; a secondary pillar only when strategically justified) · Target audience (Students / Parents / Teachers / Both) · Funnel stage · Main objective · Pain point or motivation · Tunisian Darija hook (Arabic script) · Creative concept · Recommended format · Core message · CTA · Production complexity (Low / Medium / High) · Priority score (/100) · Success KPI.
No two ideas with essentially the same message. Prioritise originality, Tunisian cultural relevance, strong hooks and clear marketing value.

## Priority score (100 points, an editorial estimate, never a performance prediction)
Business impact 30 · Audience relevance 25 · Hook strength 15 · Originality 10 · Engagement potential 10 · Production feasibility 10.
Explain why for every high-priority idea (strongest and weakest criterion). Let real performance data change the ranking. In the Orix Signals app these weights are SOSProf's scoring weights; Impact = business impact, Effort = 100 − feasibility.

## Backlog (Orix Signals → SOSProf.net → Ideas Backlog)
Fields: Content ID (tag), Title, Pillar, Audience, Objective, Format, Hook, CTA, Priority, Impact score, Effort score, Status (Idea / Draft / Production / Published), Publication date, Published URL.
Default sort: highest priority first, publishing order as tie-breaker. Filters: pillar, audience, format, status, priority, publication date. New ideas enter the Backlog undated; nothing reaches the calendar or gets published without approval. Extend the existing backlog, never rebuild it.

## Editorial calendar
Default monthly allocation 25 / 20 / 20 / 15 / 10 / 10 (pillars 01→06). These are starting targets, not fixed rules. Adapt when business priorities change, and say why:
- Teacher supply too low → temporarily raise pillar 03.
- Teacher supply strong but student registrations low → raise 01 and 02.
- Awareness strong, conversion weak → prioritise 02 and 05.
Seasonality: rentrée (September), first-term exams (early December), mid-year (March), Bac revision (April–June), Bac results (July); confirm exact dates with the user.
Posting days and times come from real audience analytics when available; otherwise label them **hypotheses to test**.

## KPIs per pillar
Measure each pillar against its own objective (table above). Never optimise for views alone: student lesson requests and qualified teacher applications are the outcomes that matter. Teacher goals and student goals are evaluated separately. Never invent analytics.

## Brand voice
Authentic Tunisian Darija in Arabic script (French school terms as Tunisians use them), natural dialogue, Tunisian situations and characters, professional educational messaging, human-centred stories, one clear benefit, strong short hooks, a relevant CTA. Brand name written exactly "SOSProf.net". Keep the existing SOSProf.net colours, logo, typography, motion, subtitle system and the Reel positioning already approved (subtitles slightly higher, top logo slightly lower); no new safe-zone redesign. Human voiceover only: every narrated idea gets a Darija recording script first.
